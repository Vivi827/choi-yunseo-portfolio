OUR LEGO WORLD!

Expanding Retail

During the past year, we grew our global network of stores, opening 155 new stores together with our partners, taking total stores globally to 904.

Expanding Supply Chain

In 2022, we continued to make progress on our capacity expansion projects for future demand, breaking ground on 2 new carbon-neutral factories in Vietnam and the United States.

Expanding to the digital world

The LEGO® Builder App grew up. There were 13.6 million downloads of the app, a 42 percent rise compared to last year.
